## What the Linux run covers, by acceptance item
- **Ungranted use is refused before start, with an actionable message:** `coordtest.PolicySuite` over the in-process and the serialized link, on the fake and on PostgreSQL. The message carries the reason code, the Capability and who can grant it.
- **Grants are recorded, revocable and attributed:** `domain` tests for `NewGrant`/`Revoke` (human Owner or Admin only, reason required, caller-supplied record fields ignored), `porttest.GrantStore` on both stores (revocation keeps the row with `revokedBy`, `revokedAt` and the reason; a stale version is a conflict; a revoked Grant cannot be brought back), and PostgreSQL tests that a direct write cannot edit what was granted, delete a Grant or reactivate a revoked one.
- **Policy properties:** `domain/policy_test.go` checks the resolution order, determinism, independence of Grant order, totality and monotonicity in denies.

Evidence level: integration-tested on Linux (fakes, plus PostgreSQL for the stores). No real agent, provider or paid API ran, and no human ran a click-through, so nothing here is real-validated.
