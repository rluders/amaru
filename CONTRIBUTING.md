# Contributing to Amaru

Amaru is at the foundation stage and is **not yet accepting external contributions**. The CLA below is in place so that the first one can be accepted. Maintainers and agents: follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), the [conventions](docs/engineering/CONVENTIONS.md) and the [delivery workflow](docs/engineering/delivery.md).

## Contributor License Agreement
Every external contribution requires agreement to the [Amaru Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) (version 1.0). It grants a license; you keep your copyright ([ADR-0026](docs/architecture/adr/0026-fair-code-community-license-with-commercial-licensing.md)). The root `LICENSE` is not yet in effect (see the [README](README.md#license)); the first external contribution is not accepted before it is.

How to sign (tooling decided 2026-10-26: manual record, no automation until external contributions start):
1. Read [CLA.md](CLA.md). If you contribute on behalf of an employer, section 4 applies to you.
2. Comment on your first pull request exactly: `I have read the Amaru CLA (version 1.0) and I agree to its terms.`
3. A maintainer verifies that the comment is from the PR author's account and adds a row to [the signature record](docs/legal/cla-signatures.md) before the PR is merged. A PR without a recorded signature is not merged.

The signature applies to your later contributions under the same CLA version. A new CLA version requires a new signature.
