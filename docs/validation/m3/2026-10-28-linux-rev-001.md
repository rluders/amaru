## What the Linux run covers
- **"Approval state survives restarts":** a Step waiting for a person, with its requested Approval, is committed; the Platform "restarts" (a new coordinator, and for PostgreSQL a new store over the same database) and `Reconcile` runs. The Step is still `awaiting_approval`, the Approval still `requested` with the same Evidence digest, and no Worker is offered it. A decision made after the restart queues the Step and a Worker is assigned; a second restart keeps the decision, and a further decision is refused.
- **"Decisions record identity, time and the Evidence shown":** the stored Approval holds the approver's id and role, the time, the rationale and the digest of the Evidence they were shown; the audit log and the Execution events carry the same.
- **"Rejection ends or reroutes per Workflow":** a rejection (rationale required) ends the Step `failed` with code `approval_rejected` and it never runs. **Reroute is not implemented.**
- **Who may decide:** contributor, viewer, no role, Agent, trigger and plugin principals (the last three even with an Owner role) are refused and change nothing; Maintainer, Admin and Owner decide.
- **Evidence:** a decision with an empty or different digest is refused.
- **Once:** a second decision, a repeated key with a different decision, and a decision on a voided Approval are refused; a repeated key with the same decision returns the recorded outcome.
- **Atomicity:** the Execution, Steps and Approvals are created in one write, and a decision moves the Approval and the Step in one write; a stale version, an Approval that gates no Step of the Execution and a duplicate id write nothing (including no audit event). In PostgreSQL a trigger refuses editing what identifies an Approval, changing a decided one, and deleting any.
- **Cancel:** cancelling withdraws a waiting Step (a new `awaiting_approval → cancelled` edge) and voids its Approval, and the Execution ends `cancelled`.
- **Scope of one Approval:** with two gated Steps, approving one starts only that one.

Evidence level: integration-tested on Linux (fakes, plus PostgreSQL for the stores). No real agent, provider or forge ran, so nothing here is real-validated.
